How to update

  1. Rocket Paste checks for updates by itself. When a new version is out, it appears in the tray menu: click the Rocket Paste icon in the system tray and choose Version 0.2 · Nightly 3 is available — Install.
  2. To check now, click the Rocket Paste icon in the system tray and choose Check for updates.

Download

Installers will be published here when the public beta starts.

Security and privacy

  • Updates are verified by signature before they install.
  • Your history stays on your computer, stored encrypted.
  • No telemetry by default.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2 — already included in Windows 11
