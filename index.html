How to update

  1. Rocket Paste checks for updates by itself. When a new version is out, you get a notification:

    Update availableVersion 0.1.0-nightly.20261024.18 is ready to install.

    Then click the tray icon and choose Version 0.1.0-nightly.20261024.18 is available — Install.
  2. To check now, click the Rocket Paste icon in the system tray and choose Check for updates.

Security and privacy

  • Updates are verified by signature before they install.
  • Your history stays on your computer, stored encrypted.
  • No telemetry by default.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2 — already included in Windows 11
